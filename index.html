vivavivu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Summary

vivavivu itself does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The game is free and may show ads between levels; those ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

What the game stores

Your progress (stars, gold, outfits, unlocked countries, sound setting) is saved on your device only. It is never sent anywhere, and it is deleted when you delete the game.

Advertising

The game may show a full-screen ad when you leave a level's result screen. Ads are never shown while you are playing a level.

When ads are on, they come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device type and system version, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown. On every device the app asks, through Apple's tracking prompt, whether ads may be personalised; if you say no, you still get ads, but not personalised ones.

The game also reads one small settings file from its own web page at launch, to know whether ads are switched on. That request carries no personal data.

What the game does not do

No accounts. No analytics of its own. No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
