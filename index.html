Crazy Runner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Summary

Crazy Runner does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

What the game stores

Your progress (stars, gold, outfits, unlocked countries, sound setting) is saved on your device only. It is never sent anywhere, and it is deleted when you delete the game.

What the game does not do

No accounts. No advertising. No analytics or tracking. No internet connection is used. No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Motion sensor

The game reads your device's motion sensor to steer. This information is used only while you play and is not recorded.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.
